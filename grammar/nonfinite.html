🧩 非谓语动词

动词不定式与动名词的固定搭配，短文填空与单项选择的必考点。

01 动词不定式 to doB1 核心 不定式可作宾语、宾语补足语、目的状语、定语。表示「目的」时最常考，常与 in order to / so as to 互换。 3 条避坑 · 11 题

不定式可作宾语、宾语补足语、目的状语、定语。表示「目的」时最常考，常与 in order to / so as to 互换。

v. + to do ｜ to do 表目的 ｜ 疑问词 + to do
用法例句
作宾语（后接 to do 的动词）want / hope / decide / plan / agree / refuse / learn / promise / wish + to do
作宾语补足语ask / tell / want / would like sb to do
表目的I went to the shop to buy some milk.
疑问词 + to doI don't know what to do / how to get there.
作定语I have something to eat.
省略 to（使役/感官）make / let / have sb do; see / hear / watch sb do
  • I want **to buy** a new bike.我想买一辆新自行车。
  • He went to the library **to borrow** some books.他去图书馆借书。（表目的）
  • My mother asked me **to clean** my room.妈妈让我打扫房间。
to 后接动词 -ing
I want to going home. I want to go home.
不定式 to 后必须接动词原形。
使役动词后多写 to
He made me to laugh. He made me laugh.
make / let / have 后接不带 to 的不定式。
不定式否定形式写错
He told me to not be late. He told me not to be late.
不定式的否定形式是 not to do，not 放在 to 前面。

02 动名词 doingB1 核心 动名词具有名词性质，可作主语、宾语。介词后必须接 doing；部分动词后只能接 doing，部分动词后接 to do 和 doing… 4 条避坑 · 11 题

动名词具有名词性质，可作主语、宾语。介词后必须接 doing；部分动词后只能接 doing，部分动词后接 to do 和 doing 意义不同。

介词 + doing ｜ v. + doing ｜ 只能接 doing 的动词需硬记
类型动词 / 结构例句
只接 doingenjoy / finish / mind / practise / suggest / avoid / keep / give upI enjoy reading.
只接 to dowant / hope / decide / plan / agree / refuse / learnI decided to go.
两者意义不同remember / forget / stop / try / go onremember doing（记得做过）vs remember to do（记得去做）
介词后be good at / be interested in / look forward to / thank you forHe is good at swimming.
作主语doing 作主语谓语用单数Reading is good for us.
固定搭配feel like doing / can't help doing / be busy doingI feel like sleeping.
  • I enjoy **listening** to music.我喜欢听音乐。
  • He is good at **playing** basketball.他擅长打篮球。
  • **Reading** is good for our mind.阅读对我们的心智有好处。
enjoy 后接 to do
I enjoy to read books. I enjoy reading books.
enjoy 后只能接动名词 -ing。
介词后接动词原形
He is interested in play football. He is interested in playing football.
介词 in 后要接动名词 -ing。
remember doing 与 to do 混淆
Remember locking the door when you leave. Remember to lock the door when you leave.
remember to do 表示「记得去做（还没做）」；remember doing 表示「记得做过」。
stop doing 与 stop to do 混淆
He stopped to smoke and felt better. He stopped smoking and felt better.
stop doing 表示「停止做某事」；stop to do 表示「停下来去做另一件事」。
