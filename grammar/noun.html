📦 名词

名词是卷面出现频率最高的词类：可数不可数、复数变化、所有格，短文填空几乎年年考。

01 可数名词复数A2 巩固 可数名词有单复数之分，复数形式分规则变化与不规则变化两类。中考最常考的是不规则复数和以 -o / -f 结尾的特殊变化。 4 条避坑 · 10 题

可数名词有单复数之分，复数形式分规则变化与不规则变化两类。中考最常考的是不规则复数和以 -o / -f 结尾的特殊变化。

规则：+s / +es ｜ 辅音字母+y → ies ｜ -f/-fe → ves ｜ 不规则：man→men, child→children
类型变化规则例词
一般情况+ sbook→books, pen→pens
s/x/ch/sh 结尾+ esbus→buses, box→boxes, watch→watches, dish→dishes
辅音字母 + yy → iescity→cities, baby→babies, story→stories
元音字母 + y直接 + sboy→boys, day→days, key→keys
-f / -fe 结尾多数 f → vesleaf→leaves, knife→knives, wife→wives, wolf→wolves
-o 结尾（有生命）+ espotato→potatoes, tomato→tomatoes, hero→heroes
-o 结尾（无生命）+ sphoto→photos, piano→pianos, radio→radios, zoo→zoos
单复数同形不变sheep, deer, fish, Chinese, Japanese
不规则变化记牢man→men, woman→women, child→children, foot→feet, tooth→teeth, mouse→mice
  • There are three **knives** and two **forks** on the table.桌上有三把刀和两把叉。
  • Two **policemen** helped the old **woman** across the street.两名警察扶着那位老妇人过马路。
  • We saw a lot of **sheep** on the hill.我们在山坡上看到许多羊。（sheep 单复数同形）
辅音字母 + y 忘变 ies
There are many citys in China. There are many cities in China.
辅音字母 + y 结尾，先把 y 变 i 再加 -es；只有元音字母 + y 才直接加 -s。
不规则复数写错
Three childs are playing with two mouses. Three children are playing with two mice.
child→children，mouse→mice，属于必须硬记的不规则变化。
sheep / deer 误加 s
There are many sheeps on the farm. There are many sheep on the farm.
sheep、deer、fish、Chinese 单复数同形，不加 -s。
-o 结尾误判有无生命
He took many photos and two potatos. He took many photos and two potatoes.
potato/tomato/hero 有生命加 -es；photo/piano/radio 无生命加 -s。

02 不可数名词与量表达A2 巩固 不可数名词没有复数形式，不能直接用 a/an 或数词修饰。表示数量要用「a piece of / a bottle of / two… 4 条避坑 · 6 题

不可数名词没有复数形式，不能直接用 a/an 或数词修饰。表示数量要用「a piece of / a bottle of / two cups of + 不可数名词」这样的单位词结构。

a/an + 单位词 + of + 不可数名词 ｜ 复数：数词 + 单位词(复数) + of + 不可数名词
中考高频不可数名词中文常见量表达
information信息a piece of information
news新闻；消息a piece of news
advice建议a piece of advice
work工作a lot of work
homework家庭作业much homework
housework家务much housework
money钱a lot of money
time时间plenty of time
water / milk / tea / juice水 / 牛奶 / 茶 / 果汁a glass of water, two cups of tea
bread / meat / rice面包 / 肉 / 米饭a piece of bread, a bowl of rice
furniture家具a piece of furniture
equipment设备a piece of equipment
luggage / baggage行李a piece of luggage
paper纸a piece of paper
music音乐a piece of music
weather天气fine weather
  • Can you give me some **advice** on learning English?你能给我一些学英语的建议吗？
  • I have **a lot of homework** to do tonight.我今晚有很多作业要做。
  • She bought **two bottles of milk** and **a piece of bread**.她买了两瓶牛奶和一块面包。
不可数名词加 -s
He gave me many advices and informations. He gave me much advice and a lot of information.
advice、information 都是不可数名词，没有复数形式，也不能用 many 修饰。
不可数名词前用 a/an
I need an advice from you. I need a piece of advice from you.
不可数名词前不能直接加 a/an，要用「a + 单位词 + of」。
单位词单复数与名词不一致
Two cup of tea, please. Two cups of tea, please.
数词修饰的是单位词，单位词要变复数，后面的不可数名词保持原形。
news 当成复数
The news are very exciting. The news is very exciting.
news 虽以 -s 结尾，但是不可数名词，谓语用单数。

03 名词所有格A2 巩固 表示「谁的」用名词所有格。有生命的名词加 's；以 -s 结尾的复数只加 '；无生命名词多用 of 结构。 3 条避坑 · 3 题

表示「谁的」用名词所有格。有生命的名词加 's；以 -s 结尾的复数只加 '；无生命名词多用 of 结构。

单数 + 's ｜ 复数以 s 结尾 + ' ｜ 不规则复数 + 's ｜ 无生命：the ... of ...
情况构成例子
单数名词+ 'sTom's bag, the boy's mother
以 -s 结尾的复数+ 'the students' books, the teachers' office
不以 -s 结尾的复数+ 'sthe children's room, men's shoes
并列名词共同拥有最后一个 + 'sTom and Jim's room（两人共用一间）
并列名词各自拥有每个都 + 'sTom's and Jim's rooms（各自的房间）
无生命名词of 结构the door of the room, the name of the film
时间 / 距离 / 国家可用 'stoday's newspaper, ten minutes' walk, China's capital
  • This is **my sister's** bike and that is **the twins'** room.这是我姐姐的自行车，那是双胞胎的房间。
  • **Children's** Day is on June 1st.儿童节在六月一日。
  • It's about **ten minutes' walk** from here.从这里走大约十分钟。
复数所有格多写 s
This is the students's reading room. This is the students' reading room.
以 -s 结尾的复数名词，所有格只加撇号，不再加 s。
children 误按 -s 复数处理
the children' toys the children's toys
children 是不规则复数，不以 -s 结尾，所有格仍加 's。
无生命名词用 's
the room's door the door of the room
无生命名词一般用 of 结构；但表示时间、距离、国家、城市时可以例外。

04 名词的词性转换（短文填空高频）B1+ 冲卓越 河北中考「短文填空」常给出形容词或动词，要求填其名词形式。核心是掌握高频后缀：-tion / -sion / -ment / -ne… 3 条避坑 · 11 题

河北中考「短文填空」常给出形容词或动词，要求填其名词形式。核心是掌握高频后缀：-tion / -sion / -ment / -ness / -ity / -ence / -ance / -er / -or / -ist。

v. + tion/sion/ment/ance/ence → n. ｜ adj. + ness/ity → n. ｜ v. + er/or/ist → 人
后缀来源例词
-tion / -ation动词 → 名词educate→education, invent→invention, invite→invitation, pollute→pollution, protect→protection
-sion动词 → 名词decide→decision, discuss→discussion, express→expression
-ment动词 → 名词develop→development, improve→improvement, achieve→achievement, agree→agreement
-ness形容词 → 名词happy→happiness, kind→kindness, sad→sadness, ill→illness
-ity形容词 → 名词able→ability, active→activity, real→reality
-ence / -ance形容词 → 名词different→difference, important→importance, silent→silence
-er / -or动词 → 人teach→teacher, visit→visitor, invent→inventor, act→actor
-ist名词 → 人science→scientist, art→artist, piano→pianist
-ing动词 → 名词meet→meeting, build→building, feel→feeling
  • We should take action to reduce air **pollution**. (pollute)我们应该采取行动减少空气污染。
  • His **invention** has changed our life. (invent)他的发明改变了我们的生活。
  • Reading brings me great **happiness**. (happy)阅读给我带来极大的快乐。
该用名词却填了动词
Air pollute is a serious problem. Air pollution is a serious problem.
句中作主语需要名词，pollute 是动词，要变 pollution。
拼写漏字母
The developement of our city is fast. The development of our city is fast.
develop + ment 时去掉词尾的 e：development，不是 developement。
happiness 拼错
Money cannot buy happyness. Money cannot buy happiness.
happy 变名词：y 变 i 再加 -ness，即 happiness。
