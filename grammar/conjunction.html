➕ 连词

河北短文填空的「盲填」题必考连词：不给提示词，要自己填 and / but / because / although 这类连接词。

01 并列连词（盲填高频）B1 核心 并列连词连接两个对等的成分。盲填时要判断前后是顺承（and）、转折（but）、选择（or）还是因果（so）。 4 条避坑 · 7 题

并列连词连接两个对等的成分。盲填时要判断前后是顺承（and）、转折（but）、选择（or）还是因果（so）。

顺承 and ｜ 转折 but / however ｜ 选择 or ｜ 因果 so / for
连词逻辑关系例句
and顺承、并列（又…又…）He is tall and strong.
but转折（但是）He is old but strong.
or选择（或者）；否则Hurry up, or you will be late.
so因果（所以）It was raining, so we stayed at home.
for因果（因为，书面）He must be ill, for he looks pale.
both ... and两者都Both Tom and Jim are here.
either ... or两者之一Either you or he is right.
neither ... nor两者都不Neither he nor I am free.
not only ... but also不仅…而且He is not only clever but also hard-working.
  • He is young, **but** he knows a lot.他年纪小，但懂很多。（转折）
  • Hurry up, **or** you will miss the bus.快点，否则你会错过公交车。（选择/否则）
  • It was raining, **so** we stayed at home.下雨了，所以我们待在家里。（因果）
转折处填 and
He is old and he is very strong. He is old but he is very strong.
前后语义相反（老 vs 强壮）要用转折连词 but。
because 与 so 连用
Because it was raining, so we stayed at home. It was raining, so we stayed at home.
because 与 so 不能同时出现，英语里只用一个连词表达因果。
although 与 but 连用
Although he is young, but he knows a lot. Although he is young, he knows a lot.
although 与 but 不能连用；中文的「虽然…但是…」在英语里只保留一个。
or 与 and 混淆（否定句）
I don't like tea and coffee. I don't like tea or coffee.
否定句中并列成分通常用 or 而不是 and。

从属连词引导从句，表达原因、让步、条件、时间、目的等关系。盲填时看主从句之间的逻辑：原因 because、让步 although、条件 if/unless、时间 when/while/until。

原因 because / since / as ｜ 让步 although / though ｜ 条件 if / unless ｜ 时间 when / while / until / as soon as
连词关系例句
because原因（直接原因）He was late because he missed the bus.
since / as原因（已知原因）Since you are here, let's start.
although / though让步（虽然）Although it rained, we went out.
if条件（如果）If it rains, we will stay at home.
unless条件（除非）You will fail unless you work hard.
when时间（当…时，可接短暂动作）When I got home, my mother was cooking.
while时间（在…期间，接持续动作）While I was reading, he was watching TV.
until时间（直到）Wait here until I come back.
as soon as时间（一…就…）I will call you as soon as I arrive.
so that目的（为了）He got up early so that he could catch the bus.
  • He was late **because** he missed the bus.他迟到了，因为他错过了公交车。
  • **Although** it was raining, he went out.虽然在下雨，他还是出去了。
  • You will fail **unless** you work hard.除非你努力，否则你会失败。
让步处填 because
Because he is poor, he is happy. Although he is poor, he is happy.
「虽然穷但快乐」是让步关系，用 although；because 表原因，逻辑不通。
条件处填 unless 但语义相反
Unless you study hard, you will pass. If you study hard, you will pass.
unless 表示「除非（否则不）」，此处应为肯定条件 if。
while 接短暂动作
While he knocked at the door, I was cooking. When he knocked at the door, I was cooking.
knock 是短暂动作，用 when；while 只能接持续性动作。
until 主句用肯定形式
He will come back until 8 o'clock. He won't come back until 8 o'clock.
「直到…才」用 not...until，主句要否定。
