01 问候与介绍B1 核心 问候与介绍有固定套语。注意正式与非正式场合的区别，以及「初次见面」与「熟人见面」的应答差异。 2 条避坑 · 3 题

问候与介绍有固定套语。注意正式与非正式场合的区别，以及「初次见面」与「熟人见面」的应答差异。

How do you do?（初次）｜ How are you?（熟人）｜ Nice to meet you.
场景问句应答
初次见面How do you do?How do you do?
熟人问候How are you?Fine, thank you. And you?
介绍自己May I introduce myself?My name is ... / I'm ...
介绍他人This is my friend, Tom.Nice to meet you.
好久不见Long time no see!Yes, it's been a long time.
告别Goodbye! / See you!See you later! / Bye!
问候近况How is everything going?Everything is fine.
  • — How do you do? — **How do you do?**——你好！——你好！（初次见面）
  • — Nice to meet you. — **Nice to meet you, too.**——很高兴见到你。——我也很高兴见到你。
  • — How are you? — **Fine, thank you. And you?**——你好吗？——很好，谢谢。你呢？
初次见面应答错误
— How do you do? — Fine, thank you. — How do you do? — How do you do?
How do you do? 是初次见面的问候，应答也用 How do you do?，不是问身体状况。
Nice to meet you 应答用 Me too
— Nice to meet you. — Me too. — Nice to meet you. — Nice to meet you, too.
固定应答是 Nice to meet you, too. / The pleasure is mine.

02 感谢、道歉与应答B1 核心 感谢与道歉都有固定应答。注意 With pleasure（乐意效劳，用于答应请求）与 My pleasure（不客气，用于回应感谢）… 2 条避坑 · 4 题

感谢与道歉都有固定应答。注意 With pleasure（乐意效劳，用于答应请求）与 My pleasure（不客气，用于回应感谢）的区别。

Thank you → You're welcome / Not at all / My pleasure ｜ Sorry → That's OK / Never mind
场景表达应答
感谢Thank you very much.You're welcome. / Not at all. / My pleasure.
感谢（较正式）I really appreciate your help.It's my pleasure.
道歉I'm sorry. / Sorry for being late.That's OK. / Never mind. / It doesn't matter.
请求原谅Please forgive me.Don't worry about it.
答应请求— Could you help me? — With pleasure.（With pleasure = 乐意效劳）
回应感谢— Thanks a lot. — My pleasure.（My pleasure = 不客气）
  • — Thank you for your help. — **You're welcome.**——谢谢你的帮助。——不客气。
  • — I'm sorry I'm late. — **Never mind.**——对不起我迟到了。——没关系。
  • — Could you open the window? — **With pleasure.**——你能开一下窗吗？——乐意效劳。
With pleasure 与 My pleasure 混用
— Thank you. — With pleasure. — Thank you. — My pleasure.
With pleasure 用于答应别人的请求（乐意效劳）；My pleasure 用于回应感谢（不客气）。
道歉应答用 You're welcome
— Sorry. — You're welcome. — Sorry. — That's OK.
You're welcome 只回应感谢；道歉的应答是 That's OK / Never mind / It doesn't matter。

03 邀请、请求与建议A2 巩固 邀请与建议有丰富的固定句式。接受用 Sure / I'd love to / Good idea，拒绝要先致谢再说明理由。 3 条避坑 · 5 题

邀请与建议有丰富的固定句式。接受用 Sure / I'd love to / Good idea，拒绝要先致谢再说明理由。

Would you like to...? ｜ How about doing...? ｜ Why not do...? ｜ You'd better do...
功能常用句式应答
邀请Would you like to come to my party?Yes, I'd love to. / Sorry, I'm afraid I can't.
邀请What about going swimming?Good idea! / Sounds great!
请求Could you please help me?Sure. / Of course. / Sorry, I can't.
建议Why not go by bus?That's a good idea.
建议You'd better see a doctor.Thanks. I will.
征求意见Shall we meet at six?OK, that's fine.
拒绝（委婉）— Can you come? — I'd love to, but I'm busy.
  • — Would you like to go with us? — **Yes, I'd love to.**——你想和我们一起去吗？——我很乐意。
  • — How about playing basketball? — **Good idea!**——打篮球怎么样？——好主意！
  • — Why not ask the teacher? — **That's a good idea.**——为什么不问问老师呢？——好主意。
Would you like to 应答用 Yes, I would
— Would you like to come? — Yes, I would. — Would you like to come? — Yes, I'd love to.
固定应答是 Yes, I'd love to.，不是 Yes, I would。
How about 后接动词原形
How about go swimming? How about going swimming?
How about / What about 后接动名词 -ing。
Why not 后接 to do
Why not to go home? Why not go home?
Why not 后接动词原形，表示建议。

04 打电话与购物问路A2 巩固 打电话有独特表达：This is ... speaking（我是…），Who's that?（你是谁？），不能说 I am ... … 3 条避坑 · 6 题

打电话有独特表达：This is ... speaking（我是…），Who's that?（你是谁？），不能说 I am ... / Are you ...? 购物与问路也有固定问答。

This is ... speaking. ｜ Who's that? ｜ Hold on, please. ｜ Can I help you?
场景表达含义
打电话-自报This is Tom speaking.我是汤姆。
打电话-问对方Who's that? / Who's speaking?你是哪位？
打电话-稍等Hold on, please. / Just a moment.请稍等。
打电话-转接Can I speak to Tom?我能和汤姆通话吗？
购物-店员Can I help you? / What can I do for you?您要买什么？
购物-顾客I'd like to buy ... / How much is it?我想买…／多少钱？
购物-试穿Can I try it on?我可以试穿吗？
问路Excuse me, how can I get to ...?请问怎么去…？
指路Go along this street and turn left.沿这条街走，然后左转。
问路-距离How far is it from here?离这里多远？
  • — Hello, **this is Tom speaking**. — Hi, Tom. This is Lily.——你好，我是汤姆。——嗨，汤姆，我是莉莉。
  • — **Can I help you?** — Yes, I'd like a cup of coffee.——您要点什么？——我要一杯咖啡。
  • — **Excuse me, how can I get to the museum?** — Go along this street.——请问博物馆怎么走？——沿这条街走。
打电话自报用 I am
— Hello, I am Tom. — Hello, this is Tom speaking.
打电话自报用 This is ... speaking，不用 I am ...。
打电话问对方用 Are you Tom?
— Are you Tom? — Who's that? / Is that Tom?
电话中问对方是谁用 Who's that?，不用 Are you ...?。
购物应答用 Yes, you can
— Can I help you? — Yes, you can. — Can I help you? — Yes, please. I'd like ...
店员的 Can I help you? 是服务用语，应答直接说需要什么。
