宾语从句有三条铁律：①用陈述语序 ②主句过去时则从句用相应过去时（客观真理除外）③连接词按句意选 that / if(whether) / 疑问词。

主句 + that/if(whether)/疑问词 + 主语 + 谓语
类型连接词例句
陈述句that（可省略）He said (that) he was busy.
一般疑问句if / whetherHe asked if I was free.
特殊疑问句疑问词He asked where I lived.
客观真理时态不变，用一般现在时The teacher said the earth moves around the sun.
主句现在时从句时态按需要He says he will come.
  • He asked me **where I lived**.他问我住在哪里。（陈述语序）
  • I don't know **if he will come**.我不知道他是否会来。
  • The teacher told us **that the earth moves around the sun**.老师告诉我们地球绕着太阳转。（客观真理用现在时）
从句用疑问语序
He asked me where did I live. He asked me where I lived.
宾语从句必须用陈述语序：疑问词 + 主语 + 谓语。
主句过去时从句仍用现在时
He said he is busy. He said he was busy.
主句是过去时，从句要用相应的过去时态（客观真理除外）。
if 与 whether 混用
I don't know if or not he will come. I don't know whether or not he will come.
与 or not 直接连用时只能用 whether。

按意义分为时间、条件、原因、结果、目的、让步、比较七类。最常考的是时间/条件状语从句的「主将从现」，以及 although 与 but、because 与 so 不能连用。

连词 + 从句, 主句 ｜ 主句 + 连词 + 从句
类型连词例句
时间when / while / before / after / until / as soon as / sinceI will call you when I arrive.
条件if / unless / as long asIf it rains, we will stay at home.
原因because / since / asHe was late because he missed the bus.
结果so ... that / such ... thatHe was so tired that he fell asleep.
目的so that / in order thatHe got up early so that he could catch the bus.
让步although / though / even ifAlthough it was raining, he went out.
比较than / as ... asHe runs faster than I do.
  • I will call you **as soon as I arrive**.我一到就给你打电话。（主将从现）
  • **If** it **rains** tomorrow, we **will stay** at home.如果明天下雨，我们就待在家里。
  • **Although** he is young, he **knows** a lot.虽然他年纪小，但他懂很多。
时间/条件从句用将来时
If it will rain, we will stay at home. If it rains, we will stay at home.
if 引导条件状语从句时，主句用将来时，从句用一般现在时（主将从现）。
although 与 but 连用
Although he is young, but he knows a lot. Although he is young, he knows a lot.
although 与 but 不能同时出现在一个句子里，二者选一。
because 与 so 连用
Because he was ill, so he didn't come. Because he was ill, he didn't come.
because 与 so 不能连用，中文的「因为…所以…」在英语里只用一个连词。
until 与 not...until 混淆
He will come back until 8 o'clock. He won't come back until 8 o'clock.
until 表示「直到…才」，主句要用否定形式 not...until。

03 定语从句B1+ 冲卓越 定语从句修饰前面的名词（先行词）。先行词指人用 who / whom / whose，指物用 which / that，关系副词 w… 4 条避坑 · 8 题

定语从句修饰前面的名词（先行词）。先行词指人用 who / whom / whose，指物用 which / that，关系副词 where（地点）/ when（时间）/ why（原因）。

先行词 + 关系代词/关系副词 + 从句
先行词关系词例句
人（主语）who / thatThe boy who is singing is my brother.
人（宾语）whom / who / that（可省略）The man (whom) I met is a doctor.
人的（所属）whoseThe girl whose bag is red is Lily.
物which / thatThe book which I bought is interesting.
地点whereThe school where I study is big.
时间whenI still remember the day when we met.
原因whyThis is the reason why he was late.
只能用 that先行词被 all/every/only/最高级/序数词修饰This is the best film that I have seen.
  • The boy **who** is singing is my brother.正在唱歌的男孩是我弟弟。
  • The book **which** I bought yesterday is interesting.我昨天买的那本书很有趣。
  • This is the school **where** my mother works.这就是我妈妈工作的学校。
指物用 who
The book who I read is good. The book which I read is good.
先行词是物时用 which 或 that，不能用 who。
地点先行词误用 which 代替 where
This is the house which I lived last year. This is the house where I lived last year.
先行词在从句中作地点状语时用 where；作宾语时才用 which。
从句中重复宾语
The book which I bought it is good. The book which I bought is good.
关系代词已代替宾语，从句中不能再出现 it。
最高级修饰先行词用 which
This is the best film which I have seen. This is the best film that I have seen.
先行词被最高级、序数词、all、only 等修饰时，只能用 that。
