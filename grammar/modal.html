🔑 情态动词

must / can / may / need 的辨析与否定回答，是单项选择的经典送分题，也是最易混淆的一组。

情态动词 + 动词原形，无人称和数的变化。不同情态动词语气与含义不同：can 表能力许可，must 表必须，may 表可以或许可，should 表应该。

主语 + 情态动词 + 动词原形 ｜ 否定：情态动词 + not + V
情态动词含义例句
can能、会（能力）；可以（许可）I can swim. / Can I use your pen?
could过去的能力；委婉请求He could swim at five. / Could you help me?
may可以（许可）；可能（推测）May I come in? / He may be at home.
must必须（主观）；一定（推测）You must finish it today. / He must be ill.
have to不得不（客观）I have to get up early.
should应该（建议）You should see a doctor.
need需要（情态或实义）Need I go? / I need your help.
had better最好（建议）You'd better not go out.
  • **Can** you **swim**? — Yes, I can.你会游泳吗？——会。
  • You **must** **wear** a helmet when riding.骑车时你必须戴头盔。
  • You **should** **drink** more water.你应该多喝水。
情态动词后加 to
I can to swim. I can swim.
情态动词后直接接动词原形，不加 to（ought to / have to 除外）。
情态动词加 -s
He cans swim. He can swim.
情态动词没有人称和数的变化。
must 否定回答用 mustn't
Must I finish it now? — No, you mustn't. Must I finish it now? — No, you needn't.
must 引导的疑问句，否定回答用 needn't / don't have to（不必）；mustn't 表示「禁止」。
mustn't 与 don't have to 混淆
You don't have to smoke here. You mustn't smoke here.
mustn't 表「禁止」；don't have to 表「不必」。

02 情态动词表推测B1+ 冲卓越 表示推测时按可能性由强到弱：must（一定）> may / might / could（可能）> can't（不可能）。 2 条避坑 · 5 题

表示推测时按可能性由强到弱：must（一定）> may / might / could（可能）> can't（不可能）。

must be（一定）｜ may/might/could be（可能）｜ can't be（不可能）
情态动词可能性例句
must非常肯定（一定）He must be at home. The light is on.
may / might / could不太肯定（可能）He may be at home.
can't否定推测（不可能）He can't be at home. I saw him at school.
must have done对过去的肯定推测He must have finished the work.
can't have done对过去的否定推测He can't have finished it so soon.
  • The light is on, so he **must be** at home.灯亮着，他一定在家。
  • He **can't be** at home. I just saw him at school.他不可能在家，我刚在学校看见他。
  • She **may come** tomorrow.她明天可能来。
否定推测用 mustn't
He mustn't be at home. I saw him. He can't be at home. I saw him.
表示「不可能」的否定推测用 can't，不用 mustn't。
有明确证据时用 may
The light is on. He may be at home. The light is on. He must be at home.
有明确证据时用 must 表示「一定」。
