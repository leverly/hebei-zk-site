01 时间介词 in / on / atB1 核心 时间介词按「范围由大到小」排列：in 用于较长时段（年、月、季节、世纪），on 用于具体某天或某天的上下午，at 用于具体时刻与短暂… 4 条避坑 · 8 题

时间介词按「范围由大到小」排列：in 用于较长时段（年、月、季节、世纪），on 用于具体某天或某天的上下午，at 用于具体时刻与短暂时间点。

in + 年/月/季节/世纪 ｜ on + 具体某天 ｜ at + 时刻/短暂时间点
介词用于例子
in年、月、季节、世纪、上下午（泛指）in 2025, in May, in summer, in the morning
on具体某一天、星期、日期、某天的上下午on Monday, on May 1st, on Sunday morning
at具体时刻、短暂时间点at 7 o'clock, at noon, at night, at breakfast
at节日（指时间点）at Christmas, at the Spring Festival
for持续一段时间for three years
since自从（接时间点）since 2020, since last year
during在…期间during the holiday
after / before在…之后 / 之前after school, before dinner
until直到until midnight
by到…为止by the end of last term
  • We will have a sports meeting **on** Friday morning.我们将在周五上午开运动会。
  • He gets up **at** six **in** the morning.他早上六点起床。
  • I have lived here **since** 2018.我自 2018 年起就住在这里。
具体某天的上下午用 in
We will meet in Sunday morning. We will meet on Sunday morning.
某一天的上下午被具体化，要用 on：on Sunday morning。
时刻用 in
The class begins in eight o'clock. The class begins at eight o'clock.
具体时刻用 at。
since 与 for 混用
I have studied English since three years. I have studied English for three years.
since 接时间点（2020），for 接时间段（three years）。
in the morning 误用 on
I read English on the morning. I read English in the morning.
泛指「在上午」用 in the morning；只有具体某天的上午才用 on。

02 介词固定搭配B1 核心 中考高频的动词 / 形容词 + 介词搭配必须整块记忆。特别注意 to 在 look forward to / be used to … 3 条避坑 · 13 题

中考高频的动词 / 形容词 + 介词搭配必须整块记忆。特别注意 to 在 look forward to / be used to 中是介词，后面接动词要加 -ing。

be + adj. + prep. ｜ v. + prep. ｜ 介词后接 doing
搭配含义例句
be good at擅长He is good at swimming.
be interested in对…感兴趣I am interested in history.
be afraid of害怕She is afraid of dogs.
be proud of为…自豪We are proud of our country.
be full of充满The box is full of books.
be famous for因…而著名China is famous for the Great Wall.
be different from与…不同My answer is different from yours.
look forward to doing期待做I look forward to seeing you.
be used to doing习惯于做He is used to getting up early.
spend ... on / (in) doing花时间做I spent two hours on my homework.
thank you for doing感谢做某事Thank you for helping me.
prefer ... to ...比起…更喜欢I prefer tea to coffee.
  • I am **looking forward to** **hearing** from you.我期待收到你的来信。
  • She is **good at** **playing** the piano.她擅长弹钢琴。
  • He **spent** two hours **on** his homework.他花了两个小时做作业。
look forward to 后接动词原形
I look forward to see you. I look forward to seeing you.
look forward to 中的 to 是介词，后面要接动名词 -ing。
be used to 后接动词原形
He is used to get up early. He is used to getting up early.
be used to 表示「习惯于」，to 是介词，接 -ing；used to do 才表示「过去常常」。
介词搭配用错
He is good in English. He is good at English.
固定搭配是 be good at，不是 in。
